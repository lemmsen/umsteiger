# Umsteiger – project context

## What it is about
Web app (PWA) for commuters in Berlin: when boarding, you should know whether to get in at the **front, in the middle or at the rear** of the train, so that at the change station you get off right at the access to your connection (e.g. S-Bahn → U8 at Gesundbrunnen). The same applies to **leaving the station**: the target is then an exit or a point tapped on the map. Goal: self-hosted, usable on the iPhone as an app via "Add to Home Screen".

First station: **Berlin Gesundbrunnen** (default: lat 52.5486, lon 13.3886, radius 450 m). Other stations by URL: `?name=…&lat=…&lon=…&r=…`

Talk to the user in **German**. **Code, identifiers and comments in English** (also file names and JSON fields); texts shown in the app live in `i18n.js` (German is the template, plus English, French, Italian, Spanish, Japanese). Notes in this repository (README, CLAUDE.md, PLAN-*.md) are in English, as the repository is public. The user is not a professional developer: explain steps briefly and concretely.

## Decisions (made on purpose)
- **Data source OpenStreetMap**: prepared in advance per station (`data/osm/<id>.json`, monthly from the Geofabrik extract); Overpass directly from the browser only as a fallback (stations by coordinates or missing file). No backend. bahn.de is **not** scraped (legally delicate, fragile, the U-Bahn side is missing there). DB APIs (RIS::Stations, FaSta) at most later as an addition, e.g. for lift outages.
- **Plain HTML/CSS/JS without a build step**, so the user can simply copy files to their server. Leaflet 1.9.4 from cdnjs for the map.
- Licence: OSM data ODbL → keep the attribution in the footer and on the map. Code: MIT (`LICENSE`).

## Files
- `core.js` – computation logic, without DOM, also runs in Node (`module.exports`), global as `window.Umstieg`
  - `stationQuery()` – Overpass query: stop positions, platforms (way/rel), stairs/escalators (`highway=steps`, `conveying`), lifts, route relations (`route=light_rail|subway|train`)
  - `buildStation()` – builds the model; long axis of the platform by principal component analysis; accesses assigned to the nearest platform (max. 12 m, ±15 m beyond the ends)
  - `lookupQuery()` / `applyLookup()` – second query: coordinates of the **previous stop** of every line
  - `finalize()` – direction of travel = vector previous stop → here, projected onto the platform axis → `frontSign`; options grouped by line/platform/direction; `ambiguous` if nearly perpendicular (|cos| < 0.35)
  - `recommend()` – access with the shortest **walk** to the target platform (`walkGraph()`/`walkRoutes()`: network of `highway=footway|path|pedestrian|corridor`, stairs +10 m and ×1.5, escalators +5 m, lifts +40 m, platforms walkable along their axis, loose ends ≤ 5 m connected to platforms or ≤ 2 m to ways – but not across levels when both sides carry a `level` (otherwise at Gesundbrunnen a footway at street level dropped onto the U8 platform three levels below); nodes identified by their position; Dijkstra from the target platform). Accesses: stairs/lifts ≤ 12 m from the platform, plus those connected to the platform by a plain footway ≤ 30 m (`linkedAccess()`, field `linked`; e.g. the stairs from Gesundbrunnen tracks 1/2 to the U8 at the end of an 11 m corridor) – so one access can serve several platforms. The starting point of an access (`anchor`) is the end reachable from the platform without steps (`anchorAtPlatform()`), no longer the one nearest to the platform edge (for stairs along the platform often the upper one); stair pieces not reachable this way that connect to other ways at their end near the platform (upper parts of stairs mapped in several pieces, stairs on another level next to the platform) are not an access of this platform. If the footway network says nothing about a platform, the assignment by distance stays. Fallback to the **straight line** if there is no way or the way is > 3 × straight line + 200 m (`r.method`); position p (0 = rear end of the train, 1 = front) → thirds "rear/middle/front"; options `liftOnly`, `override`; accesses within 15 m along the platform are grouped into "spots" (`spots`), nearly equally good spots in other thirds come back as `equal` (tolerance 5 m)
  - `applyTimetable()` – replaces the OSM lines by the timetable lines (`data/st/<id>.json`); platform by track number (only matching kind of transport, max. 40 m) → OSM route relation (same previous stop) → `route_ref` at the platform → otherwise unknown (user chooses, `withPlatform()`)
- `tools/prepare-gtfs.js` – prepares the GTFS feeds from gtfs.de (`rawdata/fv|rv|nv`) into `data/index.json` + `data/st/<id>.json` (about 50 s); merges parts of a station that are separate in the timetable (≤ 300 m and a shared name word, city names do not count; e.g. Hamburg Hbf + "HBF/Kirchenallee" + "Hauptbahnhof Nord/Süd"), the parts get a redirect file `{ mergedInto }`; `rawdata/` and `data/` are not in Git, but `data/` must go to the server
- `tools/prepare-osm.js` – filters `rawdata/germany-latest.osm.pbf` with osmium (`brew install osmium-tool`) and writes per station `data/osm/<id>.json` in Overpass format (`osm`) plus positions for `applyLookup` (`lookup`); of route relations only members with role stop*/platform*. Runs after `prepare-gtfs.js` (about 4 min, about 100 MB). Results identical to Overpass (checked at Gesundbrunnen and Jungfernstieg).
- Sections and train length: `railway:platform:section` points (A–G, 181 stations) → `pl.sections`, section of a spot = nearest point (`sectionAt()`). Stop signs (`railway:signal:stop`, captions "140 m"/"Kurzzug"/"Vollzug") → `pl.signs`; their direction is computed by `prepare-osm.js` from the track they stand on (osmium `getparents`/`getid`, tag `umsteiger:bearing`) – only in the prepared data, not in the Overpass fallback. `trainChoices()` returns selectable train lengths (short train without a length: half the platform estimated; signs behind which the train does not fit on the platform are dropped); `recommend(…, { train })` places the train at its sign and adds the walk along the platform to accesses outside the train (`offTrain`). Test `test/train.test.js`.
- `server.py` – replaces `python3 -m http.server`: serves the app and answers `/api/` from **Transitous** (`https://api.transitous.org/api/`, MOTIS with open data; since 07.11.2026, before bahn.de, which now blocks clients that are not browsers – do not work around that). Plain `urllib`, User-Agent `Umsteiger/<version> (+repository URL)` (version read from `sw.js`, which `deploy.sh` stamps), 60 s cache, a note in the log at 720 journey searches a day (Transitous asks to get in touch above that, Matrix `#transitous:matrix.spline.de`). Times converted from UTC to local time without zone (the format the app always had). Mapping: `/api/places` → `v1/geocode` (stations by stop id, addresses/places as `lat,lon`; the same station from another feed within 300 m only once), `/api/nearby` → `v6/map/stops` (box around the point; stops of one name merged, station id preferred, `de-` feeds first), `/api/journeys` → `v6/plan` (cursors as `earlier`/`later`; ids of earlier versions with `@X=…@Y=…@` are turned into coordinates), `/api/journey?id=` → `v6/refresh-itinerary`, `/api/departures` and `/api/arrivals` → `v6/stoptimes`, `/api/trip` and `/api/train` → `v6/trip` (direction of arrival from the leg geometry 300 m before the station). Modes → the product names of the app (`ICE`, `EC_IC`, `REGIONAL`, `SBAHN`, `UBAHN`, `TRAM`, `BUS` …, from bahn.de times; S/U lines filed as rail are corrected by their name). Every transit leg brings its course (`shape: { points, stops }`, simplified to 15 m), there is no `/api/shape` any more. The Berlin S-Bahn has no tracks at Transitous: the platform comes from line and direction. **No coach sequences any more** (`sequence: null`; `LIVE_PRODUCTS` in `route.js` is empty, the code for it stays). Footers of all pages: "Connections and real time: Transitous (data sources)" with links (required by Transitous) plus the OSM attribution. App: field "My train (live)" (time window −5/+15 min around "Arrival around", collapsible filter next to the label (collapsed: icons of all three kinds, deselected ones faded; expanded: multi-select buttons regional & long distance / S-Bahn / U-Bahn with counts), remembered in `umsteiger:livekinds`; U-Bahn platform via line, track or direction; stop id stored in `umsteiger:eva4:<station>`), only visible if `/api/` answers; `liveDirection()` and `mapSequence()` (DB metres → OSM axis: via the OSM sections, ≥ 2 matching letters; otherwise via the coach order – the first coach of the DB list is the front of the train, checked on 12 trains – plus direction, DB platform stretched onto the OSM platform, deviation mostly ≤ 30 m, occasionally 75 m; plausibility check with the stop signs: front ≤ 30 m from a sign = confirmed, > 50 m and mirror image ≤ 25 m = contradiction → no coach position) in `core.js`; `recommend(…, { trainRange })`. Without a coach sequence the chosen train length stays valid and the field visible. The service worker never caches `/api/`.
- **Journey search (branch `navigator`, plan and start prompt in `PLAN-NAVIGATOR.md`)**: built on bahn.de, since 07.11.2026 served by Transitous (see `server.py` above; the compact format stayed). Where the notes below say bahn.de, the data now comes from Transitous unless stated otherwise:
  - `server.py` additionally has `/api/places?q=` (stops, addresses, places from bahn.de) and `/api/journeys?from=&to=&when=&arrive=1&page=` (POST to bahn.de `angebote/fahrplan`, the answer comes gzip-compressed with status 201, `curl --compressed` is rejected with 403 → `fetch()` unpacks itself). Answer in our own compact format (legs `walk`/`transit` with `from`/`to` incl. track and real time, `prev`/`next` = stop before/after the station); walks have no coordinates at bahn.de, `fill_walks()` takes them from the neighbours or from the ids of origin/destination.
  - `core.js`: `journeyStops()` (arrival by train → `change` to the next train with ≤ 300 m walk, otherwise `leave` to the end of the walk or to the bus/tram stop), `matchStation()` (bahn.de stop → `data/index.json`, ≤ 400 m with a shared name word, otherwise ≤ 150 m), `findPlatform()` (track without "(U8)", kind of transport, line, if need be direction – rather `null` than guessing), `transferAdvice()` → `recommend()`.
  - Continuing by bus/tram: `snapStop()` moves the imprecise bahn.de coordinate of the stop to the OSM platform of the same name (the part before the city must occur in the OSM name, ≤ 150 m, bus/tram matching; of several, the bay named by bahn.de; with exactly two of the same name on both sides of the street, the right-hand one in the direction of travel to the next stop `leg.next`, otherwise the nearest; Eberswalde 862 checked against the OSM route relations (there `route=trolleybus`); bus stops with `public_transport=platform` are already in the station data, `pl.rail` false), header "For the change to bus 862 · stop Bahnhofsbrücke" (Eberswalde Hbf: bahn.de point 80 m beside the stop).

- Leaving the station: entrances (`railway=subway_entrance|train_station_entrance`) → `st.exits`; those with a letter or a name (that is not just the station name) are in `st.targets` after the platforms (`exit: true`, same label = one exit with several points). `targetOf()` turns a platform key, exit key (`exit:…`) or map point (`{ lat, lon }`, without a point `POINT_KEY` → error `needPoint`) into the target; `walkRoutes()` starts for points at the network node of the point, otherwise at the nearest **above-ground** ways (nearest + `seedSpread()`: as much as the nearest is away, at least 5, at most 30 m – if a way lies right at the point, the straight line does not jump across tracks to another one; the rest straight line, `r.tail` = length of the straight end piece, dotted on the map, from `TAIL_WARN` = 40 m note `warnTail` and distance "walk + straight line"; the footway network of the station data knows no streets and reaches only as far as the station radius (dashed circle on the map). The straight line to the target then crosses no known tracks (except on bridges/in tunnels). Overpass now requires an origin (browsers send `Origin`; `curl` without → 406) and sometimes takes 20–40 s; test `test/area.test.js`; ways with `tunnel`, `indoor`, `level` ≠ 0 or `corridor` do not count, flag `u` in `st.walk`). App: choice "My target" with groups Change / Leave the station; tapping the map sets the point (remembered in `umsteiger:point:<station>`), exits are blue dots on the map. Exits are mapped very unevenly in OSM (Jungfernstieg 25, Gesundbrunnen 2) – hence the map point. Test `test/exit.test.js`.
- Level accesses and terminal stations: `levelAccess()` makes every place where a footway on the same level leads at least 8 m away from the platform an access of kind `level` (not where a corridor to stairs begins – that is `linkedAccess()`); before, level platforms (München Hbf hall, many small stations) had no access at all. "Step-free only" (`liftOnly`) allows lifts and level accesses, the latter only if the way from there leads to the target without steps. Terminal track (`pl.deadEnd`): buffer stop (`railway=buffer_stop`, now in the Overpass query and `prepare-osm.js`) behind exactly one platform end **and** a level access at that end – buffer stops alone also stand behind through platforms (sidings at Hannover Hbf). `directionOn()` then always takes the direction towards the buffer stop, wherever the previous stop lies.
- Platforms mapped only as a point (placeholders, e.g. U Spichernstraße, U9 south; 13 stations in October 2026): `pl.estimated`. `buildStation()` assigns them no stairs/lifts (nor do they compete with real platforms for them any more), but keeps the rest in `st.spare`; `estimatePlatforms()` at the start of `finalize()` turns them into a piece of typical length (`ESTIMATED_LENGTH`: U-Bahn 100 m, S-Bahn 140 m, train 200 m) in the direction of the previous stops (stop positions are no good, they mostly lie across both tracks) and takes stairs/lifts up to 40 m beside it or beyond the ends. `recommend()` then returns `estimated: true` → note `warnEstimated`, platform dashed on the map. If the platform is added to OSM as an area, this falls away by itself. Test `test/estimated.test.js`.

- `sw.js` – service worker (app shell + map tiles offline). Every `VERSION` is **one closed set** of the app files: loaded fresh past the HTTP cache on install (`cache: 'reload'`), served only from this set (path without `?v=`), never updated piecemeal – otherwise versions mix (the iPhone showed the text key `kindsAlso`, because `app.js` was new and `i18n.js` old). A deploy therefore reaches the devices **only through a new `VERSION`**; `app.js` reloads the page once as soon as the new service worker takes over (`controllerchange`). **Nobody bumps the version by hand**: the files say `VERSION = 'umsteiger-dev'` and `?v=dev` at the files in the pages; `deploy/deploy.sh` copies the app files (`SHELL` + `sw.js`) into a staging folder, inserts a checksum of their content there and copies from there to the Pi – so every change gives a new version. Locally `server.py` sends the app files with `Cache-Control: no-cache`. For changes to the station model in `core.js` also bump `DATA_VERSION` in `core.js` (otherwise old station data stays in `localStorage`). Station list `data/index.json`: network first, offline the stored copy; Leaflet: stored once. Registers only under https. Test `test/sw.test.js` (emulates browser cache and deploy, also checks that `SHELL` matches the pages and that all files carry `?v=dev`; it reads the file list from `SHELL`).
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` – PWA/home screen
- `deploy/` – running on the user's Raspberry Pi 3 B+ (login `PI=…` and address `URL=…` in `deploy/local.sh`, the real Caddy configuration in `deploy/Caddyfile` – both only on the Mac, not in Git; the repository has `deploy/Caddyfile.example`; the repository is public, so no personal details in files under Git): Caddy serves `/srv/umsteiger/app`, obtains the Let's Encrypt certificate by itself and passes `/api/` on to `server.py` (`umsteiger.service`, only 127.0.0.1:8000). `setup-pi.sh` once with sudo, `deploy.sh` copies app + `data/` with rsync and restarts the service.
- `tools/update-data.sh` – data update in one call (download, both prepare scripts, `deploy.sh`); cleans up afterwards (timetable zips and unpacked feeds, `rawdata/osm-*.opl`), between updates only `germany-latest.osm.pbf` (4.5 GB) stays; runs on the Mac, the Pi has too little memory for it (905 MB). The user starts it by hand (monthly calendar reminder).
- Data date in the footer (`showDates()` in `app.js`): `prepared` from `data/osm/<id>.json` (date of the Geofabrik extract) and `data/st/<id>.json` (day of preparation), also in the `localStorage` cache.
